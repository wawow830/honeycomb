# Honeycomb: agreed brief

- Rethink development itself, rather than add agents to an existing workflow.
- Make it buildable with today's agents.
- Do not derive the design from one project or audience.
- Leave human participation and agent organization open.
- Establish how development would actually happen before designing the interface.

## Not yet decided

The development model, architecture, interface, and degree of autonomy remain open. Reducing human involvement is not an agreed objective; neither is requiring close human participation.

Research findings motivate hypotheses and evaluations. They do not establish user requirements or select an architecture. The existing mockup is exploratory, not an agreed design.
